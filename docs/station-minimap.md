# 2D Station Minimap HUD

Keep your bearings while walking around multi-floor space stations without opening the full map.

## Features

- **Bottom-Left Station HUD**: Automatically appears in the bottom-left corner whenever you are on foot inside a space station, and hides automatically when piloting a spaceship or when the HUD is hidden.
- **Current-Floor View**: Displays only the station floor you are currently standing on (`F1`, `F2`, etc.).
- **5×5 Module Scale**: Centered on your player marker, showing up to four `2×2` modules and surrounding `1×1` modules at once, smoothly scrolling as you walk.
- **At-a-Glance Machine & Item Icons**:
  - Shows station modules and built machines (assemblers/refineries, containers, and conveyors).
  - Displays item icons on assemblers, refineries, and containers (conveyor item icons are omitted on the minimap to keep it uncluttered).
  - Click any item icon on the minimap (or on the [Station Map](station-map.md)) to toggle that machine's icon visibility on or off across both views.
