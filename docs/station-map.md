# Full Station Map UI

View your entire space station floor-by-floor in 2D, toggle item icons, remotely build machines, and copy-paste machine layouts.

## Opening the Station Map

1. Open the regular **Map** UI.
2. Click the **Station** button in the top view-selector bar.

## Features

- **Floor-by-Floor 2D Top-Down View**:
  - Displays all station modules and every built object/machine on the active floor.
  - Use the **vertical numbered floor buttons** on the right side (`1`, `2`, `3`, …) to switch between floors.
- **Machine & Conveyor Item Icons**:
  - Assemblers, refineries, containers, and conveyor belts display icons for the item they are producing, storing, or moving.
  - **Click any item icon** to toggle its visibility on or off.
- **Remote Machine Building (`B`)**:
  - Press **`B`** (or click **Build (B)**) while in the Station Map to open the regular machine building menu.
  - Select any machine to enter placement mode, press **`R`** to rotate 90°, and click on a station module to build it remotely—**all normal inventory material requirements still apply**.
- **Single & Multi-Machine Copy-and-Paste**:
  - Click a machine to select it, **`Shift` + Click** to select multiple machines, or use **Box Select** (click two corners) to select a group of machines.
  - Click **Copy**, then click on any floor to paste the machine(s) at their relative layout (including assembler/refinery recipes). Pasting still requires and consumes the necessary materials from your inventory.
